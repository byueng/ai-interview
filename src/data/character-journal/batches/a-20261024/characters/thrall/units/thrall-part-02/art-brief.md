Create one complete horizontal 16:9 medieval leaded STAINED-GLASS artwork, one coherent scene. The ENTIRE image must be made of translucent colored glass pieces separated by clearly visible dark lead cames: faces, hands, robes, objects, terrain and sky. Lead seams cross inside faces along forehead, brows, nose bridge, cheekbones and jaw; 10-16 facial panes, not smooth painted skin inside a stained-glass border. Believable natural anatomy and subtle expression, not cartoon, not photorealistic painting. Jewel-toned handmade glass, uneven texture, directional transmitted light with readable shadows. Environment and figures one continuous perspective, no comic panels or webpage cards. Keep key faces, hands and props within central 80% of image. No text, no lettering, no readable runes, no watermark, no logos, no crosses or unrelated religious imagery.  Theme subtitle for semantic guidance only: 血脉之外，学会倾听. One continuous scene INSIDE a dim rocky CAVE: immediately after young Thrall refuses to kill the captive human boy, Grom Hellscream emerges from a hide-covered inner cave doorway and approves his courage. Thrall is a young clean-shaven green orc with BLUE eyes and black hair, plain worn unadorned clothing, empty hands. The clothed human boy remains nearby on the ground, wrists and ankles bound, dirt on clothes, no visible wounds or blood; no weapons pointed at him. Grom is taller, deep green skin, RED eyes, very prominent huge CHARCOAL-BLACK TATTOOED LOWER JAW, long black braids, many steel ear rings, RED AND BLACK LEATHER clothing with hanging iron chains. Keep Grom visually distinct from Thrall. Torchlight in cave; no outdoor tents, no blue sky, no village or landscape. Neither young Thrall's inherited armor nor Doomhammer present. Tense moral courage giving way to recognition, no sadistic spectacle.
